From account creation to first protected trade

A five-step
start.

Start in the member area, complete the IB and MT5 checks, then finish your app setup before placing a protected trade.

  1. 01

    Create your member account

    Register in the Mamba member area first. This is where you will submit verification details and manage your setup.

    Create account ↗
  2. 02

    Request IB verification

    Inside the dashboard, choose the new or existing Valetax path and submit the email linked to your broker account.

    Open member area ↗New Valetax account ↗
  3. 03

    Add your MT5 account

    After IB verification has been requested, add the MT5 account number you want Mamba to manage.

    Go to dashboard ↗
  4. 04

    Request whitelist approval

    Submit the whitelist request for that account and wait for approval before relying on Mamba to manage live positions.

  5. 05

    Download the app and start

    Install Mamba, sign in, choose V1 or V2, set your TP, SL, breakeven or trailing rules, then place trades in MT5.
